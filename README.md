# Solara Bay: Heat Rising

[![gta6-01](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![rank](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/ifulchina-ops/gta6-01-r3/rank.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)
[![reward](https://make.fun/badge/gta6-01/ifulchina-ops/gta6-01-r3/reward.svg)](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01)

An open-world crime game for the [gta6-01 bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-01): Rico rolls into Solara Bay, a sun-soaked city of its own, and runs twelve jobs to take it — including a three-part bank heist. Steal cars and drive, outrun the police under a five-star wanted system, fight the Vipers and the Kingpin, drive taxi fares, hunt hidden stashes, and switch between three radio stations while the city watches — all offline, from static files.

Play it: serve `game/` with any static server and open `game/index.html`. WASD to walk and drive, E to steal a car, click to shoot, R to change the radio, P to pause. Progress saves in the browser. Twelve story jobs, a bank heist, five wanted stars, three radio stations, one city to take.

Screenshots: `?shot=chase|heist|beach|title&seed=N` stages a scene for a photo.
